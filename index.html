FAQ

How do I reassign a job to a different technician?

Open the Dispatch Console, locate the job on the map or job list, click the job card, and select "Reassign" from the action menu. Choose the new technician and confirm.

Can technicians work without internet connectivity?

Yes. The Fieldproxy mobile app supports offline mode. Jobs synced before going offline remain accessible. Completed job data syncs automatically when connectivity is restored.

How are customers notified about their appointment?

Customers receive automated WhatsApp and SMS notifications at job creation, 24 hours before the appointment, 1 hour before, and upon technician arrival.

Who do I contact for technical support?

Reach Fieldproxy support at support@fieldproxy.in or your dedicated Furlenco Customer Success Manager.
